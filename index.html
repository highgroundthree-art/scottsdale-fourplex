01

Project overview

Program

Four homes in one building that reads from the street as a single fine house. Two-story and tri-level only. Homes run 1,008 to 1,728 SF, and the owner's home runs about 2,240 to 2,300 SF in Models 2 and 3. One home in every building is fully accessible.

Site

South Scottsdale near Old Town, inside the city's middle-housing area. No parcel is under contract yet. Each model is matched to a lot type: an 80' × 125' lot (Model 1), a deep, narrow lot (Model 2) or an 80' × 125' lot with an alley (Model 3).

Schedule

Concept design is drawn and coordinated: code analysis, life safety, site, floor plans, foundation, plumbing and electrical. Next come mechanical, roof and details, then the pro forma, lender review and the City of Scottsdale pre-application meeting.

02

Three building models

One design language, three buildings, each matched to the lot the market offers. Every model holds four fully independent homes and is sold whole to one owner-occupant.

07

Field and office communication

Questions, approvals and changes follow a written trail, so decisions made in the field are visible to the office, the lender and the building department.

  1. RFIA request for information. The field asks a question the drawings do not answer; the office answers in writing.
  2. SubmittalProduct data, shop drawings or samples sent for review before anything is ordered or installed.
  3. Change orderA written change to scope, cost or schedule, approved before the work proceeds.
  4. Field reportA dated record of progress, conditions and issues observed on site.

Investment and business model

We sell a business, not just real estate.

Read the investor proposal (12 pages)

One buyer, four homes

Each building is sold whole to one owner-occupant, who lives in one home and rents the other three. The rent from the three rentals offsets the owner's mortgage, and the owner's home stays large.

Residential financing

Four units is where residential lending ends and commercial lending begins. Owner-occupied buyers can use FHA, VA and conventional 2–4 unit programs. Terms to be confirmed with lenders.

A built-in buyer pool

VA loans allow owner-occupied purchases of up to four units, typically with no down payment. Veterans and their families are a natural first market.

Condo-ready

Separate meters, sewer cleanouts and fire-rated separations in every home keep a condominium exit open without redesign.
